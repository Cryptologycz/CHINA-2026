Visa & Entry Guide 2026

China's visa policy changed dramatically in 2025–2026. Many nationalities can now enter visa-free for 30 days. Others qualify for a 10-day transit without any paperwork. This page tells you exactly what you need before you fly.

Updated July 2026 30-Day Visa-Free 240-Hour Transit UAE · GCC · EU · US · UK
⚡ Policy updated February 2026 — UK & Canada added to 30-day visa-free list. Always verify with your local Chinese embassy before travel.

03

240-Hour Visa-Free Transit (10 Days)

240小时过境免签
ℹ️ For US Passport Holders — This Is Your Option
US citizens cannot enter China visa-free for tourism, but can use the 240-hour transit (10 days) when flying through China to a third country. Example: New York → Beijing → Tokyo → New York. You can explore Beijing, Shanghai, Xi'an and more during the 10-day layover.

The 3 Rules

1

Eligible passport (55 countries)

USA, Canada, Mexico, all EU, UK, Australia, Japan, South Korea, UAE, Qatar, Singapore, Indonesia, Brazil and more. Full list on China National Immigration Administration website (nia.gov.cn).

2

Confirmed onward ticket to a third country

China cannot be your final destination. You need a booked flight, train, or cruise to a different country departing within 240 hours. Have this printed or on your phone — immigration will check it.

3

Use a designated port of entry/exit

65 approved ports across 24 provinces — including Beijing (PEK/PKX), Shanghai (PVG), Guangzhou (CAN), Shenzhen, Chengdu, Xi'an. Tibet and Xinjiang excluded from the transit zone.

At Immigration

1

Go to the 过境 (transit) counter

Not the regular arrivals queue. Look for signs saying 过境 (guòjìng) or ask airport staff.

2

Show passport + onward ticket

Officer verifies nationality and onward booking. Processing: 10–30 minutes. Stamp shows entry date and departure deadline.

3

Register at hotel within 24 hours

Same registration rules apply — hotels handle this automatically at check-in. Apartments require PSB visit within 24 hours.

04

Tourist Visa (L Visa) — If Required

旅游签证 · L签证
📋 Apply 4–6 Weeks Before Travel
Peak periods (Canton Fair April/October, summer, Golden Week) can slow processing to 2–3 weeks. Apply at the Chinese Embassy in your country or at a CVASC (Chinese Visa Application Service Centre).

Documents Required

Valid passport (6+ months remaining), completed application form, 2 passport photos (white background, 33×48mm), flight and hotel bookings, bank statement (last 3 months), return/onward ticket. Requirements vary by nationality — confirm with your embassy.

Single vs Multiple Entry

Request double or multiple-entry if visiting Hong Kong or Macau during your trip — crossing into HK/Macau counts as leaving mainland China and requires re-entry. Single-entry is fine for a straightforward mainland visit.

Indian Passport

Streamlined since 2025 with dedicated India-China business visa counters in Delhi and Mumbai. Allow 7–10 business days. A Chinese company invitation letter significantly speeds processing for business visits.

Visa Duration vs Stay

A "30-day" visa means you can stay 30 days per entry, not 30 days from issue. The entry validity (e.g. 3 months) is how long you have to use the visa — not how long you can stay.

05

At the Border — Step by Step

入境流程
1

Arrival Card

Since late 2025 China allows online pre-completion via the National Immigration Administration website. Also available on the plane and at airport kiosks.

2

Immigration — 外国人 Lane

Use the foreigners lane (外国人 wàiguórén). Have passport, arrival card, and visa (if required) ready. Biometrics (fingerprints + photo) taken at most nationalities. Usually fast at major airports.

3

Customs Declaration

Digital via WeChat mini-program at most major airports. Declare: cash over USD 5,000, commercial goods, certain medications. Nothing to declare? Green channel. Penalties for non-declaration are serious.

4

Enable VPN Immediately

The moment you connect to any Chinese network — including airport WiFi — you lose Google, WhatsApp, Gmail. Open your pre-installed VPN before doing anything else online.

5

Hotel Registration Within 24 Hours

Hotels do this automatically at check-in. Apartments require a PSB station visit within 24 hours. Fine for non-compliance: up to ¥2,000.

⚠️ Items You Cannot Bring
Firearms and ammunition, large amounts of foreign currency (declare if over USD 5,000), fresh produce, politically sensitive material. For medications: carry a doctor's letter for anything controlled. Prescription medications in original packaging in reasonable quantities are generally permitted.
06

Extending Your Stay & Overstay Warning

延期与逾期

Visa Extension

Apply at the local Public Security Bureau Exit-Entry Administration (公安局出入境管理局) at least 7 days before expiry. Typically grants a 30-day extension. Bring passport, hotel registration, and reason for extension.

⚠️ Overstay — Serious Consequences

Fine of ¥500/day (up to ¥10,000), potential detention, and a multi-year re-entry ban. No grace period. If you're approaching your expiry date, go to PSB proactively — they are helpful to foreigners who come before expiry.
