Chinese Patent Medicines

A practical reference guide to common Chinese over-the-counter herbal remedies for cold, fever, and respiratory conditions — for foreign visitors who may encounter these at Chinese pharmacies.

⚠️ Informational purposes only. This guide does not constitute medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before taking any medication, especially for children. TCM diagnosis should be made by a licensed practitioner.
01

Common Cold: Adults

成人感冒药 · Chéngrén Gǎnmào Yào
🌿 About Chinese Patent Medicines
These are government-approved OTC (非处方药) herbal formulas sold at any Chinese pharmacy (药店 — yàodiàn). Look for the OTC label on the box. They are widely used and generally considered safe for adults when taken as directed. All are available without prescription.
01

999 Ganmao Ling Keli

999 感冒灵颗粒
999 Cold Relief Granules
Granules 颗粒
Treats
Headache Fever Nasal Congestion Runny Nose Sore Throat
One of the most popular cold remedies in China. Dissolve sachet in hot water and drink. Widely available in all pharmacies and convenience stores.
02

Lianhua Qingwen Jiaonang

连花清瘟胶囊
Lianhua Qingwen Capsules
Capsules 胶囊
Treats
Fever Muscle Aches Nasal Congestion Cough Headache Sore Throat
Broad-spectrum cold formula. Became internationally known during COVID-19. Best for colds with heat signs (fever dominant).
03

Ganmao Qingre Keli

感冒清热颗粒
Ganmao Qingre Granules
Granules 颗粒
Treats
Fever Body Aches Runny Nose Cough Headache Dry Throat
Tongrentang brand. Classic formula for wind-cold type colds. Granule form dissolves in hot water. Sugar-free version available (无蔗糖).
04

Jinhua Qinggan Keli

金花清感颗粒
Jinhua Qinggan Granules
Granules 颗粒
Treats
Fever Headache Body Aches Sore Throat Cough Nasal Congestion
Developed from a classical Han Dynasty formula. Good for influenza-like illness with wind-heat signs.
05

Banlangen Keli

板蓝根颗粒
Isatis Root Granules
Granules 颗粒
Treats
Sore Throat Dry Mouth Tonsillitis
Isatis root (板蓝根) is one of the most used herbs in TCM. Very mild, often taken as a preventive during cold season. Safe for most adults.
06

Yinqiao Jiedu Pian

银翘解毒片
Yinqiao Jiedu Tablets
Tablets 片
Treats
Fever Headache Cough Dry Mouth Sore Throat
One of the most classic TCM cold formulas. Wind-heat type cold — best when fever and sore throat are dominant symptoms. Coated tablets.
07

Xiaochaihu Keli

小柴胡颗粒
Minor Bupleurum Granules
Granules 颗粒
Treats
Fever Headache Runny Nose
Based on a 1,800-year-old classical formula. Good for alternating fever and chills. Yunnan Baiyao brand widely available.
08

Shuanghuanglian Koufuye

双黄连口服液
Shuanghuanglian Oral Liquid
Oral Liquid 口服液
Treats
Fever Cough Sore Throat
Ampoule vials. Strong heat-clearing and detoxifying formula. Wind-heat colds with fever dominant. One of China's best-selling cold liquids.
09

Pudilan Xiaoyan Pian

蒲地蓝消炎片
Pudilan Anti-inflammatory Tablets
Tablets 片
Treats
Sore Throat Tonsillitis Upper Respiratory Inflammation
Clears heat and relieves toxicity. Especially useful for throat inflammation and tonsillitis. Renhe brand widely stocked.
10

Huoxiang Zhengqi Shui

藿香正气水
Agastache Normalizing Water
Liquid 水
Treats
Nausea Vomiting Diarrhea Headache Abdominal Pain
Summer cold with digestive symptoms caused by heat-dampness. Strong smell (contains alcohol). Very effective for stomach flu-type colds. Highly recognizable small glass vials.
11

Nin Jiom Pei Pa Koa

京都念慈菴蜜炼川贝枇杷膏
Honey Loquat Syrup
Syrup 膏
Treats
Cough Sore Throat Hoarseness Excess Phlegm
Famous honey-loquat cough syrup. Made in Hong Kong. Sweet, mild, and pleasant to take. Excellent for dry cough and sore throat from cold or dryness. Often found in supermarkets.
12

Qingkailing Jiaonang

清开灵胶囊
Qingkailing Capsules
Capsules 胶囊
Treats
High Fever Sore Throat Acute Pharyngitis Acute Bronchitis
For viral colds with high fever. Stronger formula — meant for acute upper respiratory tract infections. Baishishan brand. Consult a pharmacist.

03

Understanding TCM Cold Types

风寒与风热 · Fēnghán vs Fēngrè

In Traditional Chinese Medicine, colds are categorized into two main types. Choosing the correct medicine depends on identifying which type you have — they require different treatments.

❄️ Wind-Cold (风寒 Fēnghán)

  • Chills more than fever
  • No sweating or very little
  • Clear, watery runny nose
  • Body aches and stiffness
  • No or mild sore throat
  • Thin white tongue coating
  • Often triggered by cold/wind exposure
Best medicines: Ganmao Qingre Keli, Xiaochaihu Keli

🔥 Wind-Heat (风热 Fēngrè)

  • Fever more than chills
  • Sweating present
  • Yellow or thick nasal discharge
  • Significant sore throat
  • Thirst and dry mouth
  • Yellow tongue coating
  • Often seasonal, springtime
Best medicines: Lianhua Qingwen, Yinqiao Jiedu, Shuanghuanglian
ℹ️ Not Sure Which Type? Ask the Pharmacist
At any Chinese pharmacy (药店), show the pharmacist your symptoms or use Baidu Translate camera mode to show them this text: 我是风热还是风寒感冒？ (Wǒ shì fēngrè háishì fēnghán gǎnmào?) = "Do I have a wind-heat or wind-cold cold?" They will recommend the right medicine.

At the Pharmacy in China

在中国药店购药 · Zài Zhōngguó Yàodiàn Gòuyào
🔍

Find a Pharmacy

Open Amap (高德地图) or Baidu Maps and search 药店 (yàodiàn). Major chains: 大参林 (Dà Cānlín), 老百姓大药房 (Lǎobǎixìng), 海王星辰 (Hǎiwángxīngchén). All stock OTC patent medicines.

🏷️

Look for OTC Label

OTC medicines have a green or red oval OTC sticker on the box. Green = non-prescription (safer). Red OTC = still non-prescription but requires more care. No prescription needed for any medicine in this guide.

📱

Translate the Box

Use Baidu Translate camera mode to photograph the dosage instructions (用法用量) on the back of the box. This gives you the correct dose for adults vs children, and frequency of intake.

💊

Ask the Pharmacist

Chinese pharmacies have a licensed pharmacist on duty. Show them your symptoms — they will recommend the right medicine. Say: 我感冒了 (Wǒ gǎnmào le) = "I have a cold." They will guide you.

💳

Payment

Pharmacies universally accept Alipay and WeChat Pay QR. Most also accept cash. Prices are very affordable — most OTC medicines cost ¥10–50 per box.

🌡️

When to See a Doctor

If fever exceeds 39.5°C for more than 2 days, or if symptoms worsen after 3 days of OTC treatment, visit a hospital. Go to 发热门诊 (Fārè Ménzhěn) = Fever Clinic.

⚠️ Interaction Warning
Do not combine multiple cold medicines — especially those containing paracetamol/acetaminophen (对乙酰氨基酚). Some Chinese patent medicines contain Western active ingredients alongside herbal components. Ask your pharmacist if combining medicines.

Useful Phrases at the Pharmacy

药店常用语 · Yàodiàn Cháng Yòng Yǔ
我感冒了
Wǒ gǎnmào le
I have a cold.
我发烧了
Wǒ fāshāo le
I have a fever.
我嗓子疼
Wǒ sǎngzi téng
My throat hurts / I have a sore throat.
我头疼
Wǒ tóuténg
I have a headache.
我咳嗽
Wǒ késou
I have a cough.
流鼻涕
Liú bítì
Runny nose.
鼻塞
Bísāi
Nasal congestion / blocked nose.
有没有适合外国人的药？
Yǒu méiyǒu shìhé wàiguórén de yào?
Do you have medicine suitable for foreigners?
这个儿童可以吃吗？
Zhège értóng kěyǐ chī ma?
Can children take this?
怎么服用？
Zěnme fúyòng?
How do I take this? (dosage instructions)
一天吃几次？
Yī tiān chī jǐ cì?
How many times per day?
附近有医院吗？
Fùjìn yǒu yīyuàn ma?
Is there a hospital nearby?
