China Public Holidays & What Closes

China's seven official public holidays affect travel prices, crowd levels, hotel availability, and which services are open. Knowing the calendar before you book can save significant money and stress.

2026 DatesGolden WeekSpring FestivalWhat Stays OpenBooking Strategy
⚠️ Golden Week (Oct 1–7) and Spring Festival (late Jan 2026) are the two periods foreigners most regret not planning around. Read this before booking.
01

The 7 Official Public Holidays

七个法定节假日 · 2026年
🇨🇳 How China's Holiday System Works
China has 7 official holidays totalling 11 days off. The government creates longer blocks by requiring make-up work on adjacent weekends — resulting in "Golden Weeks." During these periods hundreds of millions of people travel simultaneously. The impact on hotels, trains, and tourist sites is extreme.

Each Holiday — What to Expect

New Year's Day — 元旦

Yuándàn

A single-day holiday. Cities quickly return to normal. Major attractions, malls, restaurants and hotels all operate normally. One of the biggest retail sales periods — good for shopping.

Hotels: Normal pricesTrains: AvailableServices: Open

Visitor tip: No action needed. Book normally.

Jan–FebJan 28–Feb 37+ days

Spring Festival — 春节 ⚠️

Chūnjié · Chinese New Year 2026 = Year of the Horse 🐴

The world's largest annual human migration. An estimated 9 billion trips are made during the 40-day chunyun (春运) travel rush. Workers return to home provinces — Shanghai and Guangzhou empty out while smaller cities fill up. Most small restaurants, local shops, and small businesses close for 1–2 weeks. International hotels and chain restaurants stay open.

Hotels: +50–150% surgeTrains: Sell out 30 days aheadSmall restaurants: ClosedFlights: Premium prices

Visitor tip: Book trains on Trip.com exactly 30 days in advance (tickets release at midnight CST). Book hotels 2–3 months ahead. If you can avoid this period — do so. If you stay, embrace the temple fairs (庙会) and fireworks — it's genuinely spectacular.

AprilApr 4–63 days

Qingming Festival — 清明节

Qīngmíng Jié · Tomb Sweeping Day

Traditional ancestor-remembrance festival. Families visit graves and burn paper offerings. Cemeteries are busy but cities are otherwise normal. Some parks and scenic areas see increased domestic visitors.

Hotels: NormalTrains: Book 1 week aheadScenic spots: Busier

Visitor tip: Book trains a week early. No major disruption for foreign visitors.

MayMay 1–55 days

Labour Day — 劳动节 ⚠️

Láodòng Jié

Expanded to 5 days in recent years — now a "mini Golden Week." Popular scenic destinations (Zhangjiajie, Guilin, West Lake Hangzhou, Lijiang) become extremely crowded. Hotel prices in tourist cities spike by 30–80%.

Scenic spots: Very crowdedHotels: +30–80% tourist citiesTrains: Book 2 weeks ahead

Visitor tip: Avoid Zhangjiajie, Guilin, Lijiang during this period. Major cities (Beijing, Shanghai) are manageable. Book trains 14+ days in advance.

JuneJun 19–213 days

Dragon Boat Festival — 端午节

Duānwǔ Jié

Traditional festival celebrating the poet Qu Yuan. Dragon boat racing on rivers and lakes across China. Zongzi (sticky rice dumplings wrapped in bamboo) are eaten everywhere. A pleasant cultural event — not a major travel disruption.

Hotels: NormalServices: Mostly openRiverside areas: Crowded

Visitor tip: Watch a dragon boat race — free, spectacular, and a genuine cultural experience. Best in Guangzhou, Hangzhou, and Chengdu.

Sep–OctSep 29–Oct 13 days → 9 days in 2026

Mid-Autumn Festival — 中秋节

Zhōngqiū Jié · Moon Festival

Families gather to view the full moon and exchange mooncakes (月饼). In 2026, Mid-Autumn falls directly before National Day creating a 9-day combined holiday (Sep 29 – Oct 7) — the longest travel period of the year. Treat this as Golden Week in terms of planning.

2026: Combined = 9-day breakHotels: Book months aheadMooncakes: Sold everywhere

Visitor tip: Try a mooncake — lotus paste with egg yolk is the classic. Brilliant for gifts. Apply Golden Week booking strategy for the full Sep 29–Oct 7 block.

OctoberOct 1–77 days

National Day Golden Week — 国庆节 ⚠️⚠️

Guóqìng Jié · The Biggest Holiday of the Year

The single most impactful holiday for foreign visitors. Hundreds of millions of Chinese tourists travel simultaneously. The Forbidden City, West Lake, the Bund, Zhangjiajie, and every major scenic site becomes genuinely impassable. Train tickets sell out 30 days in advance at midnight. Hotel prices can double or triple. In 2026 this follows directly after Mid-Autumn Festival — making it a 9-day mega-holiday from Sep 29 to Oct 7.

Trains: Sold out at midnight, 30 days aheadHotels: +100–200%Major sites: ImpassableTiananmen: Flag ceremony Oct 1

If you must travel during Golden Week: (1) book hotels 3–4 months ahead, (2) buy train tickets the moment they open — midnight, 30 days before, (3) skip famous scenic spots and explore city neighbourhoods instead, (4) budget for 2× normal prices. Or better — avoid this period entirely.

03

What Stays Open vs What Closes

节假日营业情况

✅ Always Open During Holidays

International & chain hotels · Major tourist attractions · Convenience stores (7-Eleven, Family Mart, Lawson) · Supermarkets (Walmart, Carrefour) · Chain restaurants (McDonald's, KFC, Starbucks) · Shopping malls · Metro, buses, trains, airports · Hospitals and pharmacies · All digital services (Alipay, WeChat Pay, Didi, Meituan)

❌ Often Closed — Spring Festival Only

Small independent restaurants (may close 1–2 weeks) · Local barbershops and salons · Small boutiques and clothing shops · Wet markets and fresh food vendors · Small family-run businesses · Some factories (manufacturing halts) · Smaller family-run guesthouses

⚠️ Reduced Hours / Harder to Access

Banks (may close on public holidays) · Post offices · Government services including PSB registration (plan ahead — may not be open on the day you need it) · Some museums require timed tickets booked weeks ahead during Golden Week · Hospital non-emergency departments

📱 Digital Services Unaffected

Alipay and WeChat Pay work 24/7/365 · Didi ride-hailing available (surge pricing during holidays) · Meituan and Ele.me food delivery active · 12306 and Trip.com train/hotel booking · All navigation apps work normally · VPN services may be more restricted during major political dates

04

Booking Strategy for Holiday Periods

节假日预订策略
🚄

Train Tickets — The 30-Day Rule

Chinese rail tickets open exactly 30 days before departure at midnight (00:00 China Standard Time, UTC+8). During Golden Week and Spring Festival, popular routes sell out in minutes. Set an alarm, have Trip.com open and logged in, and book the moment tickets release.

🏨

Hotels — Book 2–4 Months Ahead for Peak

Good hotels in Beijing, Shanghai, Xi'an, and Chengdu book out months ahead during Golden Week. Rates triple or more. Always book free cancellation rates so you can adjust if plans change. Trip.com and Booking.com both work well for China.

🎟

Attractions — Pre-Book on WeChat

The Forbidden City, Summer Palace, and many major sites require timed-entry tickets via WeChat mini-program. During Golden Week these sell out weeks ahead. Book major attraction tickets before arriving in China, not after.

✈️

Flights — 6–8 Weeks Ahead for Peak

International flights into China during Golden Week and around Spring Festival are significantly more expensive. Domestic flights spike even more. Book both legs at least 6–8 weeks ahead if your dates overlap with any major holiday.
